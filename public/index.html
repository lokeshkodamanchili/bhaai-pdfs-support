Contribution & privacy information

Purpose and terms

Voluntary contributions support the creator’s ChatGPT Go subscription renewal, with a ₹400 fundraising goal. They do not buy a product, guarantee resource delivery, or provide a charitable tax receipt. Progress shows verified contributions less recorded refunds, before payment processing fees. Contributions above the goal support the same stated purpose; the progress bar stops at 100% and the actual total remains visible.

Privacy

Your chosen display name, contribution amount and date are public on Payments. Use “Anonymous” as your display name if preferred. Razorpay processes payment information; this site stores payment references for verification and does not store card, UPI or bank credentials. Reviews, names and ratings are public. Please avoid including sensitive personal information.

Payment issues and refunds

If a payment is debited but not listed, keep your Razorpay payment reference and contact the organiser through the project’s GitHub issues. Do not post bank details or payment credentials publicly. Refund requests are reviewed by the organiser; an approved refund is processed through Razorpay. No automatic refund is promised.
